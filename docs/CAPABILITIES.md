## Platform expansion

The owner approved a licensable enterprise agent automation platform with separately offered transformation, implementation, training, and managed services across industries. See [ADR-0009](architecture/0009-agent-automation-platform.md) and the [master plan](plans/2026-10-13-platform-master-plan.md). Studio, Control Room, execution, and assurance are capabilities of the same product. The existing Scenarios, Releases, Production Evidence, and Governance workflows remain implemented foundations.

Direction, not capability: on 2026-11-13 and 2026-11-14 the owner named supply chain operations as LotusPond Works' first market, within the cross-industry direction above. It is built to each customer's requirement, by LotusPond once or by the customer's own builders, is not limited to one process such as invoices, and includes healthcare administration workflows ([ADR-0017](architecture/0017-position-works-for-supply-chain-operations.md), [GOAL.md](GOAL.md)). No row below changes: this positioning builds or qualifies no supply chain workflow, no connection to a customer's system and no customer deployment.

Check results and any unresolved failures are recorded separately in [VALIDATION.md](VALIDATION.md); planned capability must not appear as a current product claim.

## Existing assurance implementation

On 2026-11-05 the owner retired voice sessions and the voice bridge, Gym-Anything software sandboxes, the TypeSafe Jev semantic evaluator, the model gateway, the trace-plan ladder with its monthly allowance and retention job, the Go telemetry collector and the Rust artifact verifier ([ADR-0012](architecture/0012-retire-assurance-extras.md)). Their code, routes, pages, images and CI jobs are removed; the tables that hold their stored records remain unchanged, and no code writes them. The rows below describe what remains.

On 2026-11-11 the owner retired trace ingestion over OTLP and the JSON API, monitors and trace assessments with their worker, incident reporting and state changes, signed incident alerts with their worker, receipt submission, endpoint registration (Integrations), approval requests for anything but a release, and the proposal-only License page ([ADR-0013](architecture/0013-trim-to-the-automation-core.md)). Stored traces, assessments, incidents and receipts stay readable through the API; deployment admission keeps depending on scenarios, runs, policies, releases and release approvals.

The same day, [ADR-0014](architecture/0014-focus-on-the-core-loop.md) focused the product on its core loop ([GOAL.md](GOAL.md)) and retired air-gapped operation and offline identity enrollment, the organization and installation authenticator requirements, tool authorization, the MCP and A2A runners, the Operations page and the on-premises Compose variant. Their tables stay; the lost-factor reset stays.
